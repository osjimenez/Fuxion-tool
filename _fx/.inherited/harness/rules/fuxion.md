## The Fuxion workspace

This repository is one of Fuxion's, which live together in the Fuxion workspace (`osjimenez/Fuxion-workspace`): a
metarepo that clones them inside it, each in `<name>/repo`, and holds what they share. The plans and ideas (in Spanish,
in its `_docs/`), the shared build (`_fx/dotnet/`) and the instructions for the coding agents (`_fx/harness/`) live
there. To work across repositories, open the workspace root.
